HOW DAE WORKS
01

Upload two PDFs

Drop any two documents — contracts, research papers, policy versions, textbook chapters.

02

DAE builds dual vector stores

Each PDF is split into chunks and embedded using a local model. Two FAISS indexes, one per document.

03

Cross-document similarity search

Every chunk in Doc A is matched against Doc B's vector store. High-similarity pairs go to an LLM for judgment.

04

Results with evidence

Contradictions shown side by side. Agreements confirmed. Blind spots revealed. Export as JSON or TXT.
